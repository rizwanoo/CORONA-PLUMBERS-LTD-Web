01 · Urgent Repairs

Burst Pipe Repairs

When a concealed or exposed water pipe splits, rapid containment and a durable mechanical or soldered copper repair protect your property from avoidable water damage.

Isolate & Drain: Locate the main stopcock or zonal valve and safely depressurise the affected run.

Cut-Out & Replace: Remove corroded or split sections and fit fresh copper pipework with properly supported clips.

Pressure Verification: Test the restored line under operating mains pressure before boxing back in.

02 · Fixtures & Valves

Leaking Tap & Valve Fixes

A steady drip wastes water, stains ceramic basins, and signals worn internal seals. We repair quarter-turn ceramic cartridges, traditional washers, and weeping under-sink isolation valves.

Where a tap body is beyond economical repair, we supply or fit a like-for-like or upgraded mixer cleanly without marking worktops or tiles.

Kitchen Plumbing
